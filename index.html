ABOUT RAW '26

NOT YOUR AVERAGE COLLEGE FEST.

Born out of raw passion, late-night campus brainstorming, and an unyielding disco spirit, RAW — CAMPUS UNFILTERED is the flagship cultural and technical festival of LBS Institute of Technology for Women.

For 3 days, we strip away formalities and turn our campus into an open amphitheatre of code, literary brilliance, drama, strobe lights, street art, and bass-heavy afterhours.
